About Alenaz & privacy

Alenaz is an AI assistant application created by David Vardanyan and powered by an external AI provider. Your chat history and preferences are saved in this browser, not in a cloud account. When you send messages, use web search, or upload files, that content is processed by the configured AI service. You can export your chats from Saved Chats or clear locally saved conversations here.
